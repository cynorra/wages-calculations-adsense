## Reverse Engineering Your Paycheck Stub

Many workers receive a lump-sum direct deposit notification every two weeks without knowing the exact hourly rate applied to each component of their labor. Reverse engineering a biweekly paycheck allows workers to audit employer accounting.

### Straight 80-Hour Deconstruction Formula
For standard non-exempt schedules without overtime:
`Base Hourly Wage = Gross Biweekly Pay / Hours Worked (typically 80)`

For example:
- Gross Biweekly Check: $2,400.00
- Worked Hours: 80
- `Base Hourly Rate = $2,400 / 80 = $30.00 / hour`

### Reverse Engineering with Overtime
When a biweekly check includes both regular and overtime hours:
`Gross Pay = (Regular Hours × Rate) + (Overtime Hours × 1.5 × Rate)`
`Gross Pay = Rate × (Regular Hours + 1.5 × Overtime Hours)`
`Base Hourly Rate = Gross Pay / [Regular Hours + (1.5 × Overtime Hours)]`

If an employee works 80 regular hours and 10 overtime hours and receives $2,850 gross:
- Effective Weighted Hours = 80 + (10 × 1.5) = 95 hours
- `Base Hourly Rate = $2,850 / 95 = $30.00 / hour`
- Straight time pay = 80 × $30 = $2,400
- Overtime pay = 10 × ($30 × 1.5) = $450
- Total = $2,850.00

Auditing this breakdown ensures that bonuses, holiday premiums, or automated break deductions have not improperly suppressed your legal regular rate of pay.
