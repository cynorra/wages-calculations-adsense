## Converting Accrued PTO into Cash Value

When leaving an employer, unused Paid Time Off (PTO) or accrued vacation hours often convert into a cash payout:
`PTO Payout = Accrued Unused Hours × Base Hourly Pay Rate`

For example, 80 unused vacation hours at $32.00/hour yields a gross payout of **$2,560.00**.

### State "Use-It-or-Lose-It" Prohibitions
While federal law does not require employers to provide paid vacation or pay out unused time upon termination, several states consider accrued vacation to be **vested earned wages** that cannot be forfeited:
- **California (Labor Code § 227.3):** "Use-it-or-lose-it" policies are strictly illegal. All vested vacation must be paid out in full on the final paycheck.
- **Colorado, Montana, and Nebraska:** Accrued vacation cannot be forfeited regardless of contract clauses.
- **Other States (Texas, Florida, Georgia):** Employers may enforce written forfeiture policies if explicitly stated in employee handbooks.

### The 22% Federal Supplemental Wage Withholding
Lump-sum PTO payouts are categorized by the IRS as **supplemental wages**. Under IRS Publication 15-T, employers typically withhold a flat **22% for federal income tax**, plus standard FICA (7.65%) and applicable state taxes, resulting in total deductions frequently exceeding 33% of the gross PTO check.
