## The Affordable Care Act (ACA) 30-Hour Full-Time Threshold

Under traditional office terminology, 40 hours represents full-time employment. However, for federal compliance under **Internal Revenue Code § 4980H (Employer Shared Responsibility provisions)**, an employee is legally treated as full-time if they average **at least 30 hours of service per week** (or 130 hours per calendar month).

### Schedule Breakdown for a $25.00/Hour Worker
- **20 Hours / Week (Part-Time):** $500.00/wk | $2,166.67/mo | $26,000/yr
- **25 Hours / Week (Part-Time):** $625.00/wk | $2,708.33/mo | $32,500/yr
- **30 Hours / Week (ACA Full-Time Threshold):** $750.00/wk | $3,250.00/mo | $39,000/yr
- **35 Hours / Week (Standard Corporate Full-Time):** $875.00/wk | $3,791.67/mo | $45,500/yr
- **40 Hours / Week (Standard 2,080h Schedule):** $1,000.00/wk | $4,333.33/mo | $52,000/yr

### The Value of the 30-Hour Threshold
Crossing from 29 to 30 hours per week with an applicable large employer (50+ full-time equivalent employees) triggers mandatory health insurance coverage offers. Even if nominal wages only increase by 1 hour ($25), the employer health subsidy often adds $400 to $600 per month in non-taxable fringe compensation.
