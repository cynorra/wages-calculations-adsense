## Statutory Pay Stub Requirements Under the FLSA

Under Section 11(c) of the Fair Labor Standards Act (29 U.S.C. § 211(c)) and 29 CFR Part 516, employers must maintain and provide itemized payroll records to employees. Many states (including California under Labor Code § 226) require written earnings statements containing:

1. **Gross Wages Earned:** All straight-time and overtime earnings.
2. **Total Hours Worked:** Differentiating regular hours from statutory overtime.
3. **Itemized Deductions:** Clear disclosure of all statutory taxes and voluntary deductions.
4. **Net Wages Earned:** Final net payment disbursed via direct deposit or check.
5. **Applicable Hourly Rates:** Explicit reporting of regular rate and overtime multiplier rate.

### Common Pay Stub Codes Decoded
- **FED W/H:** Federal Income Tax Withholding.
- **FICA SS / OASDI:** Federal Social Security Old Age & Survivors insurance (6.2%).
- **FICA MED:** Federal Medicare Hospital Insurance (1.45%).
- **YTD Gross:** Year-to-date cumulative gross earnings since January 1.
- **SDI / SUI:** State Disability Insurance or State Unemployment Insurance (where employee-funded).
