## Gross Rate: $80,000 Per Annum

On a standard 40-hour weekly schedule (2,080 annual hours), an $80,000 salary equals **$38.46 per hour**.

`$80,000 / 2,080 = $38.4615 -> $38.46 / hour`

- **Weekly Gross:** $1,538.46
- **Biweekly Paycheck:** $3,076.92
- **Monthly Gross:** $6,666.67

### The State Tax Impact: Texas vs. California & New York
State income taxes dramatically alter discretionary cash flow:
- **In Texas / Florida / Washington (0% State Tax):**
  - Federal Tax: ~$8,908
  - FICA (SS + Medicare): $6,120
  - State Income Tax: $0
  - **Net Annual Take-Home:** **$64,972** ($5,414 / month)

- **In California (Progressive State Brackets ~7.2% effective):**
  - Federal Tax: ~$8,908
  - FICA: $6,120
  - CA State Income Tax + SDI (State Disability Insurance): ~$5,680
  - **Net Annual Take-Home:** **$59,292** ($4,941 / month)
  - **Monthly Difference:** You take home **$473 less per month** in California than in Texas on the exact same $80,000 gross salary.

### Marginal vs. Effective Tax Rates
At $80,000 gross income, a single filer touches the 22% federal marginal tax bracket (dollars above $48,475 + $15,000 standard deduction = $63,475). However, their **effective federal tax rate is only 11.14%**, because the first $15,000 is tax-free and the next $11,925 is taxed at just 10%.
