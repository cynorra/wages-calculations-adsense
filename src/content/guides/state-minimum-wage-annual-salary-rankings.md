## The Great Minimum Wage Divergence

The federal minimum wage under 29 U.S.C. § 206 has remained at **$7.25 per hour** since July 24, 2009. Over a full-time 2,080-hour year, federal minimum wage generates:

`$7.25 × 2,080 hours = $15,080.00 gross annual salary`

In response, 30 states and the District of Columbia have enacted state statutory minimums significantly exceeding federal law:
- **District of Columbia ($17.50/hr):** $36,400.00 / year
- **Washington State ($16.66/hr):** $34,652.80 / year
- **California ($16.50/hr standard / $20.00 fast food):** $34,320.00 / year
- **New York ($16.00/hr NYC/Downstate):** $33,280.00 / year
- **Connecticut ($16.35/hr):** $34,008.00 / year

A full-time worker in Washington State earns **$19,572 more in gross wages per year** than an identical full-time worker in Texas, Georgia, or Pennsylvania earning the federal $7.25 minimum wage.
