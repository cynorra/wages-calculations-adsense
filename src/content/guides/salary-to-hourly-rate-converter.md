## The Illusion of Fixed Salaries: Understanding Wage Dilution

When salaried workers sign an employment contract for $80,000 per year, they often mentally calculate their rate using the standard 40-hour schedule:

`$80,000 / 2,080 hours = $38.46 per hour`

However, exempt employees under Section 13(a)(1) of the FLSA receive no statutory overtime pay regardless of hours worked. If demanding workplace expectations push regular work schedules to 50 hours per week, the actual hourly return erodes significantly:

`$80,000 / (50 hours × 52 weeks = 2,600 hours) = $30.77 per hour`

Working 50 hours instead of 40 represents a **20.0% reduction in real hourly compensation**, transforming an apparently lucrative compensation package into below-market return.

### Schedule Impact on an $80,000 Salary
- **35 Hours / Week:** $43.96 / hour
- **40 Hours / Week:** $38.46 / hour
- **45 Hours / Week:** $34.19 / hour
- **50 Hours / Week:** $30.77 / hour
- **55 Hours / Week:** $27.97 / hour
- **60 Hours / Week:** $25.64 / hour

### Opportunity Cost & Hourly Comparison
When evaluating a transition from an hourly non-exempt position to a salaried exempt management role, always compute the required break-even hourly rate including historical overtime hours worked.
