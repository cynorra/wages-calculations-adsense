## What is a Shift Differential?

A shift differential is premium compensation added to an employee's base hourly wage to compensate for working outside standard daytime business hours—such as second shift (evening/swing), third shift (graveyard/overnight), or weekend assignments.

### Common Industry Differential Structures
- **Healthcare (RNs, Techs):** Often a flat dollar boost of +$3.50/hr for evening shifts and +$5.00/hr for night shifts, plus an additional +$4.00/hr for weekend rotations.
- **Manufacturing & Warehousing:** Frequently a percentage premium, typically +10% to +15% over day-shift base wages.

### Mandatory Inclusion in Regular Rate for Overtime
Under FLSA principles, an employer **cannot calculate overtime solely on the base day rate** when a shift differential is earned. If a machine operator has a $20.00 base wage plus a $3.00 night differential ($23.00 total hourly rate), their overtime rate during that shift must be calculated at **$34.50/hr** ($23.00 × 1.5), not $30.00/hr ($20.00 × 1.5).
