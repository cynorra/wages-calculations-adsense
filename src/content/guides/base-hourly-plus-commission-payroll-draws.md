## Modeling Base Plus Commission Compensation

Sales compensation structures commonly combine guaranteed base hourly wages with performance incentives:
`Total Gross Pay = (Hours Worked × Base Hourly Rate) + Total Commission Earned`

For example, an account executive earning $18.00/hour who works 40 hours ($720) and closes $30,000 in monthly sales at a 5% commission rate ($1,500) generates **$2,220.00** in biweekly gross compensation ($27.75 effective hourly yield).

### Understanding "Draw Against Commission"
Many commission plans incorporate a **draw**—a guaranteed advance on future commissions:
- **Non-Recoverable Draw:** If an employee's earned commission is less than the draw amount, the employer pays the difference, and the employee is never required to repay the shortfall.
- **Recoverable Draw:** If commission earnings fall below the advance, the shortfall creates a debt that the employer deducts from subsequent commission paychecks.

### Statutory Wage Floor Protections
Regardless of commission structure or draw deficits, non-exempt sales representatives must always receive at least the statutory minimum wage for all hours worked in every pay period under FLSA regulations.
