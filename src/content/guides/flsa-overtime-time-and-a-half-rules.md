## Federal Overtime Mandate: FLSA Section 7(a)

Under the Fair Labor Standards Act (29 U.S.C. § 207), covered non-exempt employees must receive overtime compensation at not less than **one and one-half times (1.5x)** their regular rate for all hours worked exceeding 40 in a workweek.

### Standard Federal 1.5x Calculation
If an employee earns $24.00 per hour and works 48 hours in a standard workweek:
- **Straight Time Pay (40 Hours):** 40 × $24.00 = $960.00
- **Overtime Pay Rate:** $24.00 × 1.5 = $36.00 / hour
- **Overtime Pay (8 Hours):** 8 × $36.00 = $288.00
- **Total Gross Pay:** **$1,248.00**

### State Variations: California Daily Overtime & Double Time
While federal law only evaluates weekly overtime (hours exceeding 40 per week), state laws can impose stricter protections. For example, under **California Labor Code § 510**:
- Hours worked beyond 8 up to 12 hours in a single workday must be paid at **1.5x**.
- Hours worked beyond 12 in a single workday must be paid at **double time (2.0x)**.
- Any hours worked beyond 8 on the 7th consecutive day of work must be paid at **double time (2.0x)**.
