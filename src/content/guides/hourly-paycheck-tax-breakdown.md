## Anatomy of Hourly Payroll Deductions

When an hourly worker earns $35.00/hour over an 80-hour biweekly pay period ($2,800 gross), their net paycheck is shaped by statutory withholdings required under the Internal Revenue Code and state statutes.

### 1. Federal Insurance Contributions Act (FICA)
- **Social Security Tax (OASDI):** Levied at 6.2% on the first $176,100 of annual earnings. On an 80-hour check at $35/hr, exactly **$173.60** is deducted.
- **Medicare Tax (HI):** Levied at 1.45% on all earnings without limit. On a $2,800 check, exactly **$40.60** is deducted.
- **Total FICA Deductions:** **$214.20 (7.65%)**.

### 2. Social Security Wage Base Limit Cap Benefit
Workers earning over $176,100 annually reach the Social Security cap mid-year. Once cumulative year-to-date earnings exceed $176,100, the 6.2% deduction ceases immediately, increasing net take-home pay by $173.60 on every subsequent $2,800 paycheck through December 31.
